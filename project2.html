About the Career Fair

The 2026 OCVTS Tech & Cybersecurity Career Fair connects job seekers and students with industries interested in those who need it.
Note: The event will last 3 days with the same schedule each day.

Information

Date: November 14 – 16, 2026 (3-Day Event)

Time: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM EST daily (8:00 AM for booth setup)

Location: Ocean County Vocational-Technical School

Admission: Free for All Students & Job Seekers

Event Description: Explore career opportunities in network security, web development, IT support, and system administration. Bring copies of your resume.

Event Schedule

  • 8:00 AM: Recruiters arrive & set up
  • 9:00 AM: Doors Open
  • 10:30 AM: Panel for Cybersecurity
  • 12:00 PM: Resume and Portfolio Workshop
  • 1:30 PM: Technical Interviews (Stage)
  • 3:00 PM: Final Networking and Doors close
